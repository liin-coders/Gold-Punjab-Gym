03 GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Do I need to book before visiting?

No. You can walk in any day between 6 AM and 10 PM. Booking a visit simply means a coach will be ready to show you around and talk through your goals.

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. New visitors get a complimentary goal-setting consultation. No payment is needed to see the gym.

I am a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Our Beginner Foundations programme covers the basics, correct form and a plan at your pace, with a coach beside you.

How quickly will you reply?

We usually reply the same day. For the quickest answer, call us or send a WhatsApp message.
